# Uma Musume Stat System

## Core Stats

### Speed (スピード)

- **Effect**: Determines initial race positioning and sprint capability
- **Breakpoints**: 901 (B+ threshold), 1600 (S threshold)
- **Training**: Speed training provides highest base gains
- **Race Bonus**: +400 hidden stat bonus applied during races
- **Optimal Range**: 1200-1600 for most builds

### Stamina (スタミナ)

- **Effect**: Prevents speed loss in final stretch
- **Critical Threshold**: Must meet race distance requirements
- **Long Distance**: 800-1000+ required for 2400m+ races
- **Middle Distance**: 600-800 for 1800-2400m races
- **Hidden Mechanic**: Insufficient stamina causes exponential speed decay

### Power (パワー)

- **Effect**: Ability to maintain position and resist blocking
- **Positioning**: Critical for middle-pack and rear strategies
- **Terrain**: Extra important on hill courses
- **Recommended**: 800-1200 for competitive builds

### Guts (根性)

- **Effect**: Recovery rate during race and last-spurt performance
- **Late-Game**: Becomes more valuable in final 200m
- **Synergy**: Works with stamina to prevent fatigue
- **Priority**: Often 4th or 5th in training priority

### Wit (賢さ)

- **Effect**: Skill activation rate and adaptability
- **Skill Builds**: Essential for hint/skill-focused strategies
- **Activation**: Higher wit = more consistent skill triggers
- **Modern Meta**: 800-1000+ recommended for skill-heavy builds

## Stat Growth Rates

### Aptitude Modifiers

- **S Aptitude**: 1.2x growth rate
- **A Aptitude**: 1.1x growth rate
- **B Aptitude**: 1.0x growth rate (baseline)
- **C Aptitude**: 0.9x growth rate
- **D Aptitude**: 0.85x growth rate
- **E Aptitude**: 0.8x growth rate
- **F/G Aptitude**: 0.75x growth rate

### Support Card Bonuses

- Friend cards: +10% bond bonus at max friendship
- Stat-specific cards: +5-15% to related stat gains
- Rainbow bonus: +15% all stats when full deck synergy achieved
- Event bonuses: Vary by card, typically +20-50 temporary stats

## Hidden Mechanics

### Race Stat Boost

- All characters receive +400 to all stats during actual races
- This boost is NOT reflected in visible stats
- Affects race performance calculations but not training outcomes
- Critical for understanding race simulation vs visible stats

### Stat Scaling

- Stat effectiveness has diminishing returns above 1200
- 1200-1400: ~70% efficiency
- 1400-1600: ~50% efficiency
- 1600+: ~30% efficiency
- Breakpoint gains (901, 1600) still valuable despite diminishing returns

### Turn Efficiency

- First 24 turns: Highest stat gain multipliers
- Turns 25-48: Standard gains
- Turns 49-72: Reduced gains, focus shifts to skill acquisition
- URA Finals: No stat training, skills and condition management only

## Optimal Stat Distribution

### Front Runner

- Speed: 1400+ (S rank)
- Stamina: Distance-dependent (600-1000)
- Power: 800-1000
- Guts: 600-800
- Wit: 800-1000

### Late Surger

- Speed: 1200-1400
- Stamina: Distance + 100 buffer
- Power: 1000+ (critical for positioning)
- Guts: 800-1000 (late-game push)
- Wit: 800-1000

### Pace Chaser

- Speed: 1300-1500
- Stamina: Distance requirement
- Power: 900-1100
- Guts: 700-900
- Wit: 800-1000

### Balanced Build ("Betweener")

- All stats: 1000-1200
- Advantage: Versatility across distances
- Disadvantage: May lack specialization for tough races
- Best for: Multiple goal completion strategies
