# Training System Mechanics

## Training Types

### Speed Training (スピード練習)

- **Primary Stat**: Speed (+20-40 base)
- **Secondary Stat**: Power (+5-10)
- **Energy Cost**: 20-25 (varies by level)
- **Failure Risk**: Low at high mood
- **Best For**: Speed-focused builds, early game stat foundation

### Stamina Training (スタミナ練習)

- **Primary Stat**: Stamina (+20-40 base)
- **Secondary Stat**: Wit (+5-10)
- **Energy Cost**: 20-25
- **Failure Risk**: Low to medium
- **Best For**: Long-distance specialists, maintaining energy

### Power Training (パワー練習)

- **Primary Stat**: Power (+20-40 base)
- **Secondary Stat**: Speed (+5-10)
- **Energy Cost**: 25-30
- **Failure Risk**: Medium
- **Best For**: Mid-pack strategies, hill races

### Guts Training (根性練習)

- **Primary Stat**: Guts (+20-40 base)
- **Secondary Stat**: Stamina (+5-10)
- **Energy Cost**: 20-25
- **Failure Risk**: Low to medium
- **Best For**: Late-spurt builds, filling stat gaps

### Wit Training (賢さ練習)

- **Primary Stat**: Wit (+20-40 base)
- **Secondary Stat**: Skill points (+3-8)
- **Energy Cost**: 15-20 (lowest)
- **Failure Risk**: Very low
- **Best For**: Skill acquisition, consistent gains

## Support Card Participation

### Bonus Multipliers

- **1 Card Present**: 1.1x stat gain
- **2 Cards Present**: 1.25x stat gain
- **3 Cards Present**: 1.45x stat gain
- **4 Cards Present**: 1.65x stat gain
- **5 Cards Present**: 1.85x stat gain
- **Rainbow (All Types)**: +15% bonus to multiplier

### Bond Level Effects

- **Bond 0-20**: 1.0x participation bonus
- **Bond 21-40**: 1.05x participation bonus
- **Bond 41-60**: 1.10x participation bonus
- **Bond 61-80**: 1.15x participation bonus
- **Bond 81-100**: 1.20x participation bonus (MAX)

### Event Triggering

- Higher bond = higher event trigger chance
- Events can provide:
  - Temporary stat boosts (+20-100)
  - Skill hints (reduced SP cost)
  - Mood improvements
  - Energy recovery
  - Motivation increases

## Training Levels

### Level Progression

- Training facilities start at Level 1
- Max Level: 5
- Upgrade costs: 10,000 coins per level
- Level increases base stat gains by ~10% per level

### Upgrade Priority

1. **Primary Stat Training**: Focus on your build's core stat first
2. **Secondary Stat**: Support your strategy (e.g., Stamina for distance)
3. **Wit Training**: Always valuable for SP generation
4. **Balanced Approach**: Don't neglect support stats

## Failure System

### Failure Consequences

- **Stat Loss**: Lose turn with no gains
- **Energy Drain**: Still consume energy (-20 to -25)
- **Mood Penalty**: Can decrease mood by 1 level
- **Injury Risk**: Small chance of injury condition
- **Motivation Drop**: May reduce motivation level

### Failure Risk Factors

- **Low Energy**: <30% = high risk
- **Low Mood**: Poor mood = increased failure chance
- **Low Motivation**: Below "Normal" = penalty
- **High Training Level**: Higher levels = slightly higher risk
- **Condition**: Injury/poor condition increases risk

### Mitigation Strategies

- Maintain energy above 40%
- Keep mood at "Normal" or better
- Use rest/outings strategically
- Train wit when other stats risky
- Monitor failure rate indicators (! symbol)

## Energy Management

### Energy Regeneration

- **Rest**: +50-60 energy
- **Outing**: +30-40 energy + mood benefit
- **Health Room**: +60-70 energy (late game)
- **Natural Regen**: +10 per turn
- **Events**: Variable (+20 to +50)

### Energy Thresholds

- **100%**: Maximum efficiency, lowest failure risk
- **70-100%**: Safe training range
- **40-70%**: Moderate efficiency
- **20-40%**: High failure risk, consider rest
- **<20%**: Critical, forced rest recommended

## Optimal Training Patterns

### Early Game (Turns 1-24)

- Focus on 2-3 core stats
- Prioritize high support card participation
- Build bond levels for mid-game events
- Maintain energy above 50%

### Mid Game (Turns 25-48)

- Maximize stat gains with rainbow bonuses
- Balance stat training with skill acquisition
- Use facilities strategically for breakpoints
- Prepare for URA/goal races

### Late Game (Turns 49-72)

- Finalize stat goals (hit breakpoints)
- Focus on skill points and acquisition
- Maintain condition for critical races
- Strategic rest before major competitions

### URA Finals (Post-Turn 72)

- No stat training available
- Focus on condition management
- Skill learning only
- Race participation mandatory

## Advanced Mechanics

### Rainbow Bonus Timing

- Occurs when all 5 stat types represented in support deck
- Appears randomly during training
- +15% to ALL stat gains
- Duration: Single training session
- Worth delaying other actions to capitalize

### Perfect Training

- All 5 support cards present
- Rainbow bonus active
- Character at excellent mood
- High energy (80%+)
- Result: ~100-150 total stat points in one turn

### Training Link Combos

- Certain card combinations create special events
- Link events provide bonus stats + unique skills
- Require specific card pairings and bond levels
- Research deck synergies for optimal links
