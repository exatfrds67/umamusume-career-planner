## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Navigate to the registration page
2. Enter your email address and create a password
3. Verify your email (if required)
4. Log in to access your dashboard

### First-Time Setup

After logging in for the first time:

1. **Set your preferences** - Choose your preferred language and theme (light/dark mode)
2. **Create your first character** - Start by adding a character to manage
3. **Build your support deck** - Select 6 support cards for your character
4. **Start a career** - Begin your first training run

## Character Management

### Creating a Character

1. Click "New Character" from the dashboard or characters page
2. Enter the character name
3. Select the scenario type:
   - **URA Finale** - Standard training scenario
   - **Unity Cup** - Team-based scenario with Spirit Burst mechanics
4. Set initial stats (optional)
5. Click "Create Character"

### Character Stats

Each character has five main stats:

| Stat | Description | Impact |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **Speed** | Running speed | Final stretch performance |
| **Stamina** | Endurance | Ability to maintain pace |
| **Power** | Acceleration | Position changes, hill climbing |
| **Guts** | Mental strength | Performance under pressure |
| **Wit** | Intelligence | Race strategy, skill activation |

### Aptitudes

Aptitudes determine how well your character performs in different conditions:

**Distance Aptitudes:**

- Sprint (1000-1400m)
- Mile (1400-1800m)
- Medium (1800-2400m)
- Long (2400m+)

**Surface Aptitudes:**

- Turf
- Dirt

**Running Style Aptitudes:**

- Front Runner (逃げ)
- Pace Chaser (先行)
- Late Surger (差し)
- End Closer (追込)

Aptitude grades range from G (lowest) to S (highest, maximum grade).

### Managing Characters

- **Edit** - Update character information and stats
- **View Details** - See full character profile with history
- **Delete** - Remove a character (this cannot be undone)

## Career Planning

### Starting a Career

1. Select a character
2. Choose the scenario type
3. Optionally set career goals
4. Click "Start Career"

### Career Phases

A career consists of three phases:

1. **Junior Phase** (Turns 1-24)
   - Focus on building base stats
   - Limited race options
   - Establish training patterns

2. **Classic Phase** (Turns 25-48)
   - Main competitive period
   - Access to major races
   - Skill acquisition focus

3. **Senior Phase** (Turns 49-72)
   - Final stat optimization
   - Championship races
   - Legacy building

### Career Goals

Set goals to track your progress:

- Target stat totals
- Specific races to win
- Skills to acquire
- Aptitude improvements

## Training System

### Training Types

| Type | Primary Stat | Secondary Stats |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| Speed | Speed | Power |
| Stamina | Stamina | Guts |
| Power | Power | Stamina |
| Guts | Guts | Speed, Power |
| Wit | Wit | Speed |
| Rest | - | Recovers energy |

### Training Mechanics

**Stat Gains:**

- Base gain depends on training level
- Support card bonuses increase gains
- Friendship bonuses from high friendship levels
- Mood affects all gains

**Energy Management:**

- Training consumes energy
- Low energy increases failure risk
- Rest recovers energy
- Mood affects energy recovery

**Training Failures:**

- Occur when energy is low
- May cause injuries
- Injuries reduce stats temporarily

### Training Predictions

The AI-powered prediction system shows:

- Expected stat gains for each training type
- Failure probability
- Recommended training based on your goals

## Race Management

### Race Types

**By Grade:**

- **G1** - Championship races (highest prestige)
- **G2** - Major races
- **G3** - Important races
- **OP** - Open races
- **Pre-OP** - Beginner races

### Race Entry

1. View available races for the current turn
2. Check race requirements (distance, surface, grade)
3. Review your character's aptitudes
4. Enter the race

### Race Results

After a race:

- View finish position
- Earn SP rewards
- Gain fans (affects future opportunities)
- Unlock achievements

### Race Strategy

Consider these factors:

- **Aptitude Match** - Higher aptitudes = better performance
- **Stat Requirements** - Each race has optimal stat ranges
- **Running Style** - Match your character's strengths
- **Competition** - Stronger fields in higher grades

## Support Card Deck

### Deck Composition

Your deck consists of 6 support cards:

- 5 regular cards (positions 1-5)
- 1 friend card (position 6)

### Card Types

| Type | Primary Bonus | Best For |
|------|---------------|----------|
| Speed | Speed training | Speed-focused builds |
| Stamina | Stamina training | Long-distance runners |
| Power | Power training | Acceleration builds |
| Guts | Guts training | Pressure situations |
| Wit | Wit training | Strategic runners |
| Friend | Various bonuses | Flexible support |

### Card Rarity

- **R** - Common cards
- **SR** - Rare cards
- **SSR** - Super rare cards (strongest)

### Meta Tiers

Cards are ranked by effectiveness:

- **S+** - Top tier, highly recommended
- **S** - Excellent choices
- **A** - Strong options
- **B** - Decent alternatives
- **C** - Basic options

### Deck Optimization

Use the deck analyzer to:

- Check stat coverage
- Identify synergies
- Find gaps in your deck
- Get replacement suggestions

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Q: My stats aren't updating after training**
A: Refresh the page or check your internet connection. If the issue persists, contact support.

**Q: OCR isn't recognizing my screenshot**
A: Ensure the screenshot is clear and shows the full character status. Try adjusting brightness/contrast.

**Q: AI Advisor isn't responding**
A: The AI service may be temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few minutes.

**Q: I can't start a new career**
A: Check if you have an active career for that character. Complete or abandon it first.
