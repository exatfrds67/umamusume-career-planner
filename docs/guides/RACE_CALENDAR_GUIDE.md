## Overview

Uma Musume: Pretty Derby career runs span **72 turns** across three in-game years.
Effective career planning requires knowing when mandatory races occur, when to schedule optional races for fan acquisition,
and when to reserve turns for intensive training before key events.

Failing to meet fan count thresholds before mandatory races causes the career to end early,
so fan management is a hard constraint on training plans.

---

## Turn Structure

| Period | Turns per Month | Months | Total Turns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Year 1 | 2 | 12 | 24 |
| Year 2 | 2 | 12 | 24 |
| Year 3 | 2 | 12 | 24 |
| **Total** | | | **72 turns** |

Each month has two turns: **Early** (first half) and **Late** (second half).
Most races and events are anchored to a specific month and half, so knowing the calendar lets you predict exactly which
turns are race turns vs training turns.

### Turn Allocation Summary

- ~15–20 turns are consumed by mandatory and recommended races
- ~5–10 turns are seasonal events (training camps, special events)
- ~42–52 turns are available for free training, optional races, and rest

## Career Stages

| Stage | Years | Months | Key Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-Debut | Year 1 | April – May | Character introduction; no races yet |
| Junior | Year 1 | June – Year 1 December | First races unlocked; Junior-tier leagues |
| Classic | Year 2 | January – Year 2 December | Classic-tier races; Triple Crown events |
| Senior | Year 3 | January – March (+ scenario climax) | G1/Finale races; Championship scenarios |

Stage transitions automatically advance the available race pool — Junior-only races close after Year 1,
Classic races close after Year 2, and the Senior stage culminates in the scenario-specific finale race.

## Race Tier System

Races are ranked from lowest to highest prestige:

| Tier | Grade | Fan Reward | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OP (Open) | Ungraded | Low | Early fan acquisition; recommended for low-fan starts |
| Listed | Listed | Low–Medium | Stepping stone toward graded races |
| G3 | Grade 3 | Medium | First graded tier; good mid-Junior fan boost |
| G2 | Grade 2 | Medium–High | Mid-Classic races |
| G1 | Grade 1 | High | Pinnacle races; required for inheritance farming |
| Scenario Goal | Special | Very High | URA/Unity Cup final races; scenario-specific |

Higher-tier races require higher stat totals and fan counts to enter.
Winning G1 races also benefits the character's inheritance value for future generations.

## Mandatory vs Optional Races

### Mandatory Races

Mandatory races are assigned by the selected career scenario. Failing to enter them ends the career early.

- **URA Finale scenarios**: Three qualifying rounds (Finals A, B, C) and then the URA Finale itself
- **Unity Cup scenarios**: Four-character team races with fixed event months
- Check the scenario briefing at career start to identify all mandatory race turns

### Optional Races

Optional races are purely voluntary. They provide:

- Fan count increases
- SP (Skill Points) from race winnings
- Bond level increases with support cards who match the race's stat type

### Race Scheduling Priorities

1. **Never miss mandatory races** — always check the qualifying threshold fan count before the mandatory event month
2. **Use OP/Listed races early in Junior** to build fans cheaply (low SP cost, low stat requirement)
3. **Win at least 2+ G1 races in Classic** for inheritance farming value and fan milestones
4. **Reserve the turn before a major G1** as either a rest turn or a light optional race for bond maintenance

## Seasonal Training Camps

### Summer Training Camp

The largest and most impactful seasonal event:

| Period | Turns | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer Camp | Early July – Late August | 4+ turns of enhanced training with bonus stat gains |

- Summer camp provides higher base stat gains than regular training
- Bond bonuses also increase during camp — excellent for pushing support cards toward friendship threshold
- Camp events can trigger special support card events not available during regular training
- **Strategy**: Enter summer camp with as high energy as possible; rest the turn immediately before camp starts

### Other Seasonal Events

- **New Year (January Early)**: Minor stat bonus and fan event
- **Golden Week (May Early)**: Light bond event with support cards
- **School cultural festivals**: Scenario-specific events in Junior year (URA only)

## Fan Count Requirements

Fan counts gate entry into higher-tier races and scenario advancement. Each scenario has specific thresholds.

### General Fan Milestones

| Race Tier Unlock | Approximate Fans Needed |
| --- | --- |
| OP → Listed | 1,000+ |
| Listed → G3 | 5,000+ |
| G3 → G2 | 20,000+ |
| G2 → G1 | 50,000+ |
| G1 → Scenario finale | 100,000–300,000+ (scenario-dependent) |

> These are approximate values; exact thresholds vary by character and scenario.
> Check the in-game requirements screen as you approach each threshold.

### Fan Growth Strategy

- Run OP/Listed races in the first 2–3 months of Junior (June–August Year 1) to establish fan baseline
- Win one G3 in October–November Year 1 before the December G1 to ensure fan eligibility
- Never skip races entirely across a 3-month block or you will fall behind fan thresholds

## Race Distance and Stat Alignment

Match your training plan to the distances of races you intend to run.

| Distance Range | Primary Stats | Stamina Needs | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint (1000–1400 m) | Speed, Power | Low | Quick burst races; Wit activation matters more |
| Mile (1400–1800 m) | Speed, Guts | Moderate | Balanced races; most common Classic distance |
| Medium (1800–2200 m) | Speed, Stamina, Power | Moderate–High | Core G1 distances (Yushun, Shuka Sho) |
| Long (2200–2800 m) | Stamina, Power, Speed | High | Japan Cup, Arima Kinen; stamina is decisive |
| Extended (2800+ m) | Stamina, Guts | Very High | Tennoushou Spring (3200 m); guts prevents fade |

If your character has a Speed aptitude but your target races are Long distance, you need to supplement Stamina heavily
to avoid running out of steam before the final straight.

## Race Planning Strategy

### Turn Budget Template

```text
Year 1 (24 turns):
  Races: 4–6 turns (debut + 3 optionals + 1 G1)
  Summer Camp: 4 turns (July–August)
  Training: 14–16 turns
  Rest: 0–2 turns (only if energy critically low)

Year 2 (24 turns):
  Races: 6–8 turns (Triple Crown + 2–3 optionals)
  Inspiration Event 2: 1 turn (late March)
  Training: 14–16 turns
  Rest: 1–2 turns (pre-G1 recovery)

Year 3 (24 turns):
  Races: 8–10 turns (Senior G1 circuit + scenario finale)
  Inspiration Event 3: 1 turn (late March)
  Training: 10–13 turns
  Rest: 2–3 turns (pre-scenario finale recovery)
```text

### Key Decision Points

- **July Year 1 (Early)**: Start of summer camp — enter with full energy
- **November Year 1 (Early)**: Last training window before December G1s — prioritise your weakest aptitude stat
- **February Year 2 (Late)**: Last major training sprint before Classic Triple Crown begins in April
- **Late August Year 2**: Summer camp ends — use September/October for optional G2 races before Shuka Sho
- **March Year 3 (Late)**: Final Inspiration Event — confirm parent compositions are ready
- **October–December Year 3**: Heavy race schedule; minimal training; rest between G1s
