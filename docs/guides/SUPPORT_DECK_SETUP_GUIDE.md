## Overview

After creating your character, the next critical step is **selecting and optimizing your support deck**. A well-chosen deck provides 15–30% stat bonuses and unlocks friendship training for additional power spikes during your career.

This guide walks you through the deck selection workflow and best practices for different scenarios.

---

## Step 1: Access Your Character's Deck

**From the Characters Page:**

1. Navigate to **Characters** in the main menu
2. Click on the character you just created to view their **Career Plan**
3. Look for the **Support Deck** section in the left sidebar or tabs

**From the Character Detail Page:**

- Scroll down to the **Support Deck Management** section
- Click **"Edit Deck"** or **"Select Deck"** button

---

## Step 2: Understand Your Deck Slots

Your deck always contains **exactly 6 support cards:**

| Slot | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slot 1–5** | **Owned Cards** | Cards from your collection |
| **Slot 6** | **Borrowed Card** | A friend's card (friend support) |

**Key Points:**

- You can have 0–5 owned cards (to a maximum of 5)
- 1 borrowed card slot is always reserved
- Total deck = 5 owned + 1 borrowed = 6 cards

---

## Step 3: Choose Your Deck Composition

### Strategy 1: Focused Primary Stat (URA Finale)

**Best For**: Maximizing one stat (e.g., Speed-focused build)

**Composition:**

- **4 cards** of primary stat (Speed)
- **1 card** of secondary stat (Stamina for distance)
- **1 borrowed** card (flexible)

**Example:**

```text
🔵 Speed Card (SSR) - Limit Break 2
🔵 Speed Card (SR) - Limit Break 1
🔵 Speed Card (SR) - Limit Break 0
🔵 Speed Card (R) - Limit Break 0
🟠 Stamina Card (SR) - Limit Break 1
👥 Borrowed Card (SSR Pal)
```

**Expected Bonuses:**

- +4 × 5% = +20% from card presence
- +10% × 1.2LB + 7% × 1.2LB + 7% × 1.0LB + 5% × 1.0LB = ~35% from rarity/LB
- Friendship training at turn 60: +20% additional multiplier
- **Total: up to 55% stat gain boost by late career**

### Strategy 2: Balanced Distribution (All-Arounder)

**Best For**: Flexible characters, avoiding weak stats, exploration

**Composition:**

- **1–2 cards** per stat type
- **1 borrowed** card for flexibility

**Example:**

```text
🔵 Speed Card (SSR)
🟠 Stamina Card (SR)
🔴 Power Card (SR)
🩷 Guts Card (R)
💚 Wit Card (R)
👥 Borrowed Card (pal)
```

**Expected Bonuses:**

- +6 × 5% = +30% from full deck presence
- No heavy specialization bonus, but consistent across all training types
- Friendship training is harder to activate (needs 3+ cards with same specialization)
- **Total: ~35% stat gain boost, spread across all stats**

### Strategy 3: Event-Driven (Unity Cup)

**Best For**: Leveraging story moments and character-specific interactions

**Composition:**

- **3–4 cards** that have **story events** with your character
- **1–2 cards** of secondary stats
- **1 borrowed** card strategically chosen

**Example for a specific character:**

```text
Card with "Sports Festival" event
Card with "Birthday" event
Card with "Part-Time Job" event
Card with secondary stat bonus
Secondary stat bonus card
👥 Borrowed Card with team race bonus
```

**Expected Bonuses:**

- Lower direct stat bonuses (~25%)
- Unlocks special story moments
- Potential for unique event triggers (skill gains, relationship boosts)
- Better for **narrative-focused** and **team-dynamic** gameplay

## Step 4: Assign Cards to Your Deck

### Method A: Drag and Drop (Recommended)

1. Open the **Support Deck Builder**
2. Left panel shows your **Card Inventory**
3. Right panel shows your **Active Deck (6 slots)**
4. **Drag cards** from inventory to slot and drop
5. See real-time predictions of bonuses

### Method B: Search and Selection

1. Use the **Search Box** to find specific cards
2. Filter by:
   - **Specialization** (Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, Wit, Friend)
   - **Rarity** (R, SR, SSR)
   - **Limit Break Level** (0–4 stars)
3. Click **"Add to Deck"** button
4. Cards automatically fill available slots

### Method C: Deck Templates (Quick Start)

1. Click **"Use Template"** button
2. Choose from pre-built templates:
   - **"Speed Focused"** – 4 Speed, 1 Stamina, 1 Pal
   - **"Balanced Build"** – 1 per stat, 1 Pal
   - **"Defensive"** – emphasizes Stamina/Guts
   - **"Aggressive"** – emphasizes Speed/Power
   - **"My Favorites"** – uses your 6 most-leveled cards
3. Deck auto-fills with template suggestions
4. Adjust as needed

Current Status:

## Step 6: Confirm and Save Deck

1. Review the summary panel one final time
2. Click **"Confirm Deck"** or **"Save Changes"**
3. System confirms: "Deck successfully saved"
4. Deck becomes **active** immediately for:
   - Training predictions
   - AI advisor recommendations
   - Bond tracking

## Deck Management During Career

### View Current Deck Status

Any time during your career:

1. Open your **Character's Career Plan**
2. Sidebar shows **Active Deck Overview**:
   - Card names and rarities
   - Current bond levels for each card
   - Which cards are "Rainbow ready" (bond ≥80)
   - Upcoming friendship training activation

### Swap or Modify Deck (Post-Creation)

#### Option 1: Quick Card Swap

- Click card slot → select replacement → confirm
- Useful if you want same deck composition, different card

**Option 2: Rebuild Deck** (Less common)

- Click **"Edit Deck"** → redesign → save
- Warning: Resets bond levels to 0 on new cards
- Use only if necessary

#### Option 3: Use Different Template

- Click **"Apply Template"** → choose preset
- Useful for Unity Cup scenario preparation

## Optimization Tips

### Maximum Power (Late-Game Friendship)

**Goal:** Reach 3+ cards at bond ≥80 by turn 70

**Strategy:**

1. Choose 3–4 core cards of same specialization
2. Use consistently throughout career (every relevant training)
3. Monitor bond progression (sidebar widget)
4. By turn 50–60, expect 3 cards approaching bond 80
5. By turn 70+, friendship training is active = +20% multiplier

**Return:**

```text
Without friendship: 30 stat gain per training
With friendship training (3 cards): 30 × 1.2 = 36 stat gain
Over 30 trainings late-game: +180 stats from friendship bonus alone
```

### Balanced Growth (Avoiding Weak Stats)

**Goal:** No stat significantly falls behind

**Strategy:**

1. Select 1–2 cards per stat type
2. Ensure each stat has at least 1 card supporting it
3. Rotate training types consistently
4. Use borrowed card slot for flexibility

**Return:**

- No stat falls more than 100 points behind others
- Better prepared for diverse race types
- More forgiving if you need to pivot strategy mid-run

### Cost-Effective (Limited Card Pool)

**Goal:** Get good bonuses without high-rarity cards

**Strategy:**

1. Limit break R and SR cards multiple times
2. Focus on 3–4 core cards; don't try to use 6 different SSRs
3. Use borrowed pal cards to fill gaps
4. Synergy matters more than individual rarity

**Return:**

```text
R card with 4 LBs: 5% × 1.4 = 7% bonus
SR card with 2 LBs: 7% × 1.2 = 8.4% bonus
3 such cards: ~22% bonus (close to SSR deck)
```

## Common Questions

### Q: Can I change my deck after starting a career?

**A:** Yes, but with caveats:

- New cards reset bond level to 0
- Old cards stay in your inventory if removed
- Only recommended if you realize a critical mistake early (turn 1–10)
- After turn 30, stick with your deck for friendship training consistency

### Q: What if I don't have 6 good cards?

**A:** You can have fewer than 6 cards:

- Minimum: 1 owned card (+ 1 borrowed = 2 total)
- Works fine; you just get smaller bonuses
- Use borrowed card to supplement (often a high-rarity pal card)
- Focus on **limit breaking your available cards** instead of chasing rarity

### Q: How do I find cards with story events for my character?

**A:** In the Deck Builder:

1. Click **"Event Filters"**
2. Select your character
3. System shows **"Has story event with [Character]"** badge
4. Sort by events to see triggering cards first

### Q: Do borrowed cards count toward friendship training?

**A:** Yes! A borrowed pal card:

- Counts toward the 3+ card threshold for friendship training
- Contributes to multi-card bonuses (+5% per card)
- Starts at bond 0 (must build like owned cards)
- If friend limits breaks the card, you get the bonus too
