## Overview

Below is a detailed explanation of the turn-based calendar system in Career Mode of *Uma Musume:
Pretty Derby* (JP and Global servers).
This covers how the calendar works, how turns map to dates, and how races are scheduled across Pre-
Debut, Junior, Classic, and Senior years, including examples of real races that appear on specific
dates.

## 1. Structure of the Turn-Based Calendar

Career Mode uses a fixed turn-based calendar to simulate a racehorse career.

Key structure:

- A full career run lasts 3 in-game years
- Each year is divided into months
- Each month has two turns:
  - Early month
  - Late month

Therefore:

- 12 months x 2 turns = 24 turns per year
- Total career turns is approximately 72 turns

Source: [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/umamusume-pretty-derby-best-special-week-build-stats/)

Each turn represents half a month of in-game time, and the player chooses one action (training, race, rest, and so on).

This creates the strategic challenge of planning training around upcoming races and objectives.

## 2. Career Timeline Overview

The full calendar is divided into four stages:

| Stage | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-Debut | Training before first race |
| Junior Year | Year 1 (young trainee races) |
| Classic Year | Year 2 (major national races begin) |
| Senior Year | Year 3 (highest-level races) |

The calendar is inspired by real Japanese horse racing seasons, so many race names correspond to real G1 events.

Source: [Umamusume Wiki - G1 Races Calendar](https://umamusu.wiki/Game%3AG1_Races_Calendar)

## 3. Pre-Debut Period

The career begins in the Pre-Debut stage, where the trainee is not yet eligible for official races.

Typical gameplay during this period:

- Training stats
- Increasing bond with support characters
- Raising energy efficiency
- Preparing for the debut race

### Debut Race

The first official race is the Junior Make Debut, which usually occurs around:

- Junior Year - Late June

Example career objective:

- Turn 12: Junior Make Debut race

Source: [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/umamusume-pretty-derby-best-special-week-build-stats/)

This race unlocks access to most other race categories.

## 4. Junior Year (Year 1 Calendar)

The Junior Year represents the early racing stage of the trainee.

Most races during this year are lower-tier races for young competitors.

Typical race types include:

- Pre-Open races
- Open races
- Early graded races (G3 and G2)
- Limited G1 races

Source: [Deltia's Gaming - Optional Career Races Guide](https://deltiasgaming.com/umamusume-pretty-

## 6. Senior Year (Year 3 Calendar)

The Senior Year is the final year of Career Mode.

At this stage:

- Training stats are usually near their peak
- Races become more difficult
- Most competitions are G1 races

Example Senior Year race objectives include:

| Month | Race |
| --- | --- |
| Late March | Osaka Hai |
| Late April | Tenno Sho (Spring) |
| Late June | Takarazuka Kinen |
| Late October | Tenno Sho (Autumn) |
| Late November | Japan Cup |
| Late December | Arima Kinen |

Examples of these races appear in many career objectives.

Source: [LootBar](https://lootbar.gg/blog/en/umamusume-gold-city-autumn-cosmos-guide.html)

These races represent the highest level of competition in the career simulation.

## 7. Race Categories in the Calendar

All races on the calendar belong to a tier system.

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-Open | Beginner races |
| Open | Moderate difficulty |
| G3 | Entry-level graded races |
| G2 | High-level races |
| G1 | Top-tier championship races |

G1 races give the largest rewards, including:

- Large fan increases
- Trophies
- Skill points
- Items

Source: [Deltia's Gaming - Optional Career Races Guide](https://deltiasgaming.com/umamusume-pretty-
derby-optional-career-races-guide/)

## 8. Fan Requirements and Race Eligibility

Some races have entry requirements, such as minimum fan counts.

Example:

- Certain races require thousands of fans before entry
- Players may run optional races to reach these requirements

This system encourages balancing training and racing.

## 9. Optional vs Objective Races

The calendar includes two kinds of races.

### Mandatory (Career Objectives)

These races must be completed to continue the scenario.

Examples:

- Debut race
- Major story races
- Specific G1 events

### Optional Races

Players can also enter additional races.

Benefits include:

- Gaining fans
- Earning skill points
- Improving spark generation for inheritance

## 10. Seasonal Training Periods

Certain calendar periods temporarily change gameplay.

Example: Summer Training Camp

Occurs roughly:

- Early July to Late August

During this period:

- Support cards appear more frequently
- Training gains increase
- No normal races occur

This period is often used for major stat boosts.

## 11. End-of-Career Race Timing

Most career scenarios end after the final major races of the Senior Year.

The final calendar events usually occur around:

- Senior Year - Late December

After this point, the scenario transitions to its final championship races.

## 12. Strategic Calendar Planning

Experienced players plan around the calendar by:

- Scheduling key races in advance
- Avoiding races during important training periods
- Targeting specific G1 races to gain fan counts and inheritance bonuses

Winning many unique G1 races improves the quality of the character as a Veteran parent for inheritance.

Source: [Umamusume Wiki - G1 Races Calendar](https://umamusu.wiki/Game%3AG1_Races_Calendar)

## Summary

The turn-based calendar system structures the entire Career Mode progression.

Key mechanics:

| Mechanic | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Turn system | 1 turn = half a month |
| Total turns | Approximately 72 per career |
| Stages | Pre-Debut -> Junior -> Classic -> Senior |
| Race scheduling | Fixed calendar dates |
| Race tiers | Pre-Open -> Open -> G3 -> G2 -> G1 |
| Seasonal phases | Training camps and major races |

The calendar creates a strategic scheduling system, where players must decide when to train, rest,
or race in order to reach peak performance for important competitions.
