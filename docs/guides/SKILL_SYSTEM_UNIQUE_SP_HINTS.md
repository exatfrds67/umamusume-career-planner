## Overview

Below is a detailed expansion of the skill system mechanics in *Uma Musume: Pretty Derby*, focusing specifically on:

1. Unique skills (character signature abilities)
2. Skill cost mechanics (Skill Points economy)
3. Skill hint system and cost-reduction mechanics

This explanation expands on the previously described skill activation system and categories.

## 1. Unique Skills (Character Signature Abilities)

### 1.1 Definition

Every playable trainee in *Uma Musume: Pretty Derby* possesses a Unique Skill (often called the
character's "ultimate" skill).

Characteristics:

- Exclusive to a specific character
- Stronger than most normal skills
- Typically designed around that character's running style and race strategy

Unique skills are classified as a separate skill type within the system and are considered powerful
race abilities unique to each trainee.

Source: [Umamusume Wiki - Game:Skills](https://umamusu.wiki/Game%3ASkills)

Example effects include:

- Large speed boosts during final sprint
- Acceleration bursts exiting corners
- Stamina recovery under specific race conditions

### 1.2 Unique Skill Activation Mechanics

Unique skills activate only when specific race conditions are met.

Typical activation conditions include:

| Condition Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Position | Top 3 at final corner |
| Race phase | Last 200 meters |
| Running style | Only while using Late Surger |
| Distance | Only in medium races |

The race simulation continuously checks whether these conditions are satisfied.

If the condition is met:

- The unique skill activates automatically

Players must therefore build characters and skill sets that reliably create those conditions.

Source: [Game8 - How to Increase Unique Skill Level](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537920)

Example:

- A skill requiring 3rd-5th position in final corner will rarely activate if the runner constantly leads the race.

### 1.3 Unique Skill Strength Compared to Normal Skills

Unique skills typically provide stronger effects than regular skills.

Reasons:

1. Higher speed or acceleration modifiers
2. Longer durations
3. Special animation triggers during races

Many builds treat the unique skill as the centerpiece of the character's race strategy.

Supporting skills are chosen specifically to ensure the unique skill activates consistently.

### 1.4 Unique Skill Level System

Unique skills have levels, which determine their effectiveness.

Level increases occur through two main methods.

#### Method 1: Star Rank Upgrades

Increasing a character's star rarity upgrades their base unique skill.

Example progression:

| Star Level | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 3-star | Unlock improved unique skill |
| 4-star to 5-star | Further increase skill level |

Star upgrades require Star Pieces, obtained through duplicates or rewards.

Source: [Game8 - How to Increase Unique Skill Level](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537920)

#### Method 2: Career Milestone Upgrades

During Career Mode, the unique skill level can increase if certain conditions are met, usually involving:

- Fan count milestones
- Scenario goals

These upgrades temporarily increase the power of the skill during the run.

### 1.5 Inherited Unique Skills

Unique skills can also be inherited through green sparks in the inheritance system.

However, inherited versions have limitations:

- They are weaker than the original
- They cannot be upgraded during Career Mode

Despite this limitation, inherited unique skills are valuable because they add additional powerful abilities to a build.

Source: [Game8 - How to Increase Unique Skill Level](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537920)

## 2. Skill Points (SP) and Skill Cost Mechanics

### 2.1 Skill Point Currency

Skills are acquired by spending Skill Points (SP).

Players earn SP during Career Mode through:

- Training sessions
- Events
- Races
- Scenario milestones

Source: [GameWith - How to Get Skill Points](https://gamewith.net/uma-musume/article/show/69468)

Example sources:

| Source | SP Gain |
| --- | --- |
| Training | Small amount |
| Wit training | Larger SP gain |
| Races | Moderate to large gain |
| G1 race victory | Very high gain |

Players begin a career with a small initial SP pool and accumulate more over time.

Source: [GameWith - How to Get Skill Points](https://gamewith.net/uma-musume/article/show/69468)

### 2.2 Skill Cost Structure

Every skill has a base SP cost.

Typical cost ranges:

| Skill Rarity | Typical SP Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Normal skills | Approximately 120 to 180 SP |
| Advanced skills | 200+ SP |
| Gold skills | 300+ SP |

More powerful skills generally require more SP.

Source: [Gamezebo - Uma Musume Skills Explained](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/uma-musume-skills/)

Because SP is limited during a run, choosing which skills to purchase is a major strategic decision.

### 2.3 Opportunity Cost in Skill Purchasing

Since SP is limited:

- Buying many weak skills may reduce the ability to buy powerful gold skills later
- Saving SP until late in the career allows players to optimize purchases

This creates a resource-management layer within Career Mode.

Players often delay buying skills until:

- Late Senior Year
- After all hints are collected

## 3. Skill Hint System

### 3.1 What Skill Hints Are

Most skills cannot be purchased until the player receives a skill hint.

Hints reveal the skill and allow it to be purchased with SP.

Source: [Umamusume Wiki - Game:Skills](https://umamusu.wiki/Game%3ASkills)

Skill hints are typically obtained from:

| Source | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Support card events | Training with card |
| Scenario events | Story triggers |
| Inheritance sparks | Legacy skills |

The hint system is therefore deeply tied to support decks and career events.

Source: [Xbox Play - How Do Skills Work in Umamusume](https://xboxplay.games/umamusume/how-do-
skills-work-in-umamusume-65511)

### 3.2 Hint Levels

Hints have levels, which reduce the SP cost of learning the skill.

Cost reduction formula:

| Hint Level | Discount |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | -10% |
| Level 2 | -20% |
| Level 3 | -30% |
| Level 4 | -35% |
| Level 5 | -40% |

Thus a skill can become up to 40% cheaper.

Source: [uma.guide - Skills Explained](https://uma.guide/guides/skills/)

Example: Base skill cost = 200 SP

| Hint Level | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| None | 200 |
| Level 1 | 180 |
| Level 3 | 140 |
| Level 5 | 120 |

This is why players often delay purchasing skills until high hint levels are obtained.

### 3.3 Sources of Skill Hints

Hints can appear in several ways.

#### 1. Training Hints

When a training option shows a red exclamation mark, it means a support card present will give a skill hint.

#### 2. Event Hints

Support cards have event chains that reliably give hints.

#### 3. Scenario Hints

Story events may grant hints tied to the scenario.

#### 4. Inheritance Hints

Legacy characters can provide hints through inheritance sparks.

Source: [Xbox Play - How Do Skills Work in Umamusume](https://xboxplay.games/umamusume/how-do-
skills-work-in-umamusume-65511)

## 4. Skill Hint Pools

Each support card has a skill hint pool.

This means a support card can potentially give multiple skills.

Example:

| Support Card | Skill Pool |
| --- | --- |
| Card A | 4 possible skills |
| Card B | 14 possible skills |

Cards with smaller pools provide more consistent access to specific skills.

Source: [uma.guide - Skills Explained](https://uma.guide/guides/skills/)

## 5. Strategic Interaction Between Skills, Costs, and Hints

The skill system forms an economic loop:

1. Players earn SP throughout the career
2. Skill hints unlock skills and reduce costs
3. Players decide when to purchase skills based on available SP and hint levels

This creates several strategic approaches.

### Early Purchase Strategy

Buy key skills early for race success.

Pros:

- Helps win difficult races

Cons:

- Fewer SP for late-game skills

### Late Purchase Strategy

Save SP until the end of the career.

Pros:

- Maximum hint discounts
- Optimized skill build

Cons:

- Weaker performance earlier

## 6. Interaction Between Unique Skills and Normal Skills

Most builds treat the unique skill as the central mechanic.

Supporting skills are chosen to:

- Create the activation conditions
- Improve race positioning
- Maintain stamina until the final phase

Example synergy:

```text
Mid-race positioning skill
      ->
Corner acceleration
      ->
Unique skill final burst
```

This layered design is why skill selection strongly affects race outcomes.

## Summary

The skill subsystem in *Uma Musume* combines several interacting mechanics.

| Mechanic | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Unique skills | Powerful character-specific abilities |
| Skill Points (SP) | Currency used to learn skills |
| Skill hints | Unlock skills and reduce cost |
| Hint levels | Up to 40% SP discount |
| Skill pools | Determine hint availability |
| Unique skill upgrades | Increase skill power |

Together these systems form the core build-crafting system of the game, allowing players to design
specialized racers for different race strategies.
