# Umamusume: Pretty Derby - Trailblazer Career Mode Research

**Research Date:** March 25, 2026  
**Global Release Date:** March 12, 2026 (UTC)

---

## Overview

"Trackblazer: Start of the Climax" (known as "Make A New Track!!" / MANT in the Japanese version) is the third permanent career scenario introduced to the global English version of Umamusume: Pretty Derby. It was released on March 12, 2026 at 10:00 PM UTC, alongside the Kitasan Black, Matikane Tannhauser, and Narita Top Road (Speed SSR) banners, as well as the "Flying Run-Up!" Story Event and 1st Anniversary Celebration Part 2.

This scenario represents a significant departure from previous career modes, introducing a sandbox-style system focused on race participation and shop mechanics rather than fixed race goals. It is a **permanent scenario** and will remain accessible even after additional scenarios are released.

---

## Key Differences from Previous Scenarios

| Feature | URA Finale | Unity Cup | Trailblazer |
|---------|------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Goal System** | Fixed race goals | Team races + Spirit Burst | Result Points (no fixed goals) |
| **Final Event** | URA Finals (3 races) | Unity Cup | Twinkle Star Climax |
| **Shop System** | None | Limited | Pro Shop with extensive items |
| **Training Focus** | Facility training | Spirit Burst + Team building | Race-focused progression |
| **Rivals** | Standard | Standard | Special Rivals with skill hints |
| **Stat Caps** | 1200 all | 1200 all | 1200/1900/1200/1200/1500 |
| **Career Goals** | Trainee-specific | Team-based | Year-end Result Points |
| **Secret Events** | Available | Available | **DISABLED** |

### Important Changes from Previous Scenarios

1. **No Secret Events:** Unlike URA Finale and Unity Cup, secret events tied to specific trainees do NOT trigger in Trackblazer. For example:
   - Silence Suzuka cannot acquire Runaway style
   - Mejiro Ardan cannot be inflicted with Glass Legs
   - Gold City and King Halo avoid their difficult career events
   - Narita Taishin never has to deal with her "Don't Say It!" massive mood drop

2. **No Race Goals:** Trainees don't have their own goal races, allowing complete freedom in race selection.

3. **No Scenario Links:** Unlike Unity Cup's character-specific bonds, there are no scenario links required.

4. **Higher Stat Potential:** Significantly higher stat potential than previous scenarios due to Pro Shop items and race rewards.

## Core Mechanics

### 1. Result Points System

**Goal:** Earn a determined amount of Result Points by the final turn in December each year.

- **Failure Condition:** Playthrough ends if you don't accumulate enough points by year-end
- **Requirements:** Increase based on trainee's year and aptitudes
- **Reset:** Result Points reset every time you accomplish a Career goal
- **ALL Grade Points are consumed by Career Goals** and do NOT carry over to the next year

#### Year-End Requirements

| Date | Turf Requirement | Dirt Requirement |
|------|------------------|------------------|
| Late June, Junior Year | Debut Race | Debut Race |
| End of Junior Year | 60 Grade Points | 30 Grade Points |
| End of Classic Year | 300 Grade Points | 200 Grade Points |
| End of Senior Year | 300 Grade Points | 300 Grade Points |

**Note:** Sprint Umas with poor Aptitudes in other Distances also follow the Dirt Grade Point Requirements.

### 3. Pro Shop System

The Pro Shop is exclusive to Trackblazer and offers a wide variety of items. The Shop is unlocked after the Debut Race, and refreshes every 6 turns. Races can add additional items to the shop, which last 3 turns. After a Shop refresh, new Items have a chance to be discounted.

### 4. Rivals System

- **Indicated by:** Red and blue jagged "VS" bubble
- **Mechanic:** Tougher opponents appear in certain races randomly after Early August Junior Year
- **Reward:** Defeating a rival (1st place) grants skill hints or +5 to 2 random Stats
- **Skill Hints:** Based on race distance and running style used to win
- **Comparison:** Similar difficulty to Legend Races - only 1st place counts as a win
- **Note:** If neither party places first, it's a draw and no skill hint is awarded

### 5. Twinkle Star Climax

The scenario's climactic finale replacing URA Finals:

- **Structure:** Three-race tournament at the end of Senior Year
- **Goal:** Accumulate Victory Points across all three races
- **Champion:** Highest Victory Points total wins as "Twinkle Top Star"
- **Race Variety:** Short, Mile, Classic, Long, and Dirt distances
- **Determination:** Race distances determined by races won during career

**Victory Points Scoring:**

| Placement | Victory Points |
|-----------|----------------|
| 1st | 10 |
| 2nd | 8 |
| 3rd | 6 |
| 4th | 4 |
| 5th-6th | 3 |
| 7th-9th | 2 |
| 10th-13th | 1 |
| 14th+ | 0 |

**Note:** The Twinkle Star Climax consists of 3 Training Turns and 3 Race Turns. Save ~150 Coins for the final Shop as TS Climax Races do not reward Coins.

#### How to Unlock Specific TS Climax Distances

To unlock a specific distance for the Twinkle Star Climax, you must run predominantly in that distance during your career:

- **Long TS Climax:** Run in Long-distance races (Tenno Sho Spring, Kikuka Sho, Arima Kinen, Hanshin Daishoten, Nikkei Sho, etc.)
- **Dirt TS Climax:** Focus on Dirt races
- **Sprint TS Climax:** Focus on Sprint races

The races available will be based on your most frequently participated distance during the three years.

## Recommended Race Schedules

Trackblazer features a flexible race scheduling system via the Agenda Planner. Below are optimized schedules for different distance/aptitude combinations.

### Turf: Mile to Long (Highest Stat Gains)

**Bonuses:** +270 Stats, Homestretch Haste hint +1

This schedule provides the most epithets and highest stat rewards. Increase Mile aptitude to A to maximize gains.

**Recommended Umamusume:** Gold Ship, Manhattan Cafe, Matikane Fukukitaru, Mejiro McQueen, Mayano Top Gun, Narita Brian, Special Week, Symboli Rudolf, T.M. Opera O, Tamamo Cross

### Turf: Mile to Medium (Triple Tiara Route)

**Bonuses:** +210 Stats, Mile Straightaway ◯ hint +1

Limited race options but focuses on the prestigious Triple Tiara.

**Recommended Umamusume:** Any filly capable of Mile-Medium

### Turf: Sprint to Long

**Bonuses:** +300 Stats, Mile Straightaway ◯ hint +1

Modified Mile to Long with 2 Sprint G1s. Best for Umamusume with aptitudes across all distances like King Halo, Maruzensky, and Mihono Bourbon.

### Turf: Sprint to Medium

**Bonuses:** +280 Stats, Mile Straightaway ◯ hint +1

Plenty of race options with multiple epithets available.

### Dirt/Turf: Sprint to Medium (Mixed)

**Bonuses:** +330 Stats, Mile Straightaway ◯ hint +1

Highest stat gain schedule. Requires good Turf aptitude.

**Recommended Umamusume:** Taiki Shuttle, Haru Urara (with Turf sparks)

### Dirt/Turf: Sprint and Mile

**Bonuses:** +290 Stats, Mile Straightaway ◯ hint +1

Best for Taiki Shuttle due to her default aptitudes. Save cupcakes for end-of-year mood management.

### Dirt: Sprint to Medium (Dirt Only)

**Bonuses:** +230 Stats, Top Pick hint +1

Straightforward rotation for Dirt specialists. Consider inheriting Turf sparks for flexibility.

**Recommended Umamusume:** Smart Falcon, Haru Urara

### Triple Tiara + Long Route

**Bonuses:** +260 Stats, Homestretch Haste & Mile Straightaways Hint

Hybrid route for fillies capable of both Triple Tiara and Long distances.

## Deck Building Strategy

### Speed + Wit Framework (Recommended)

**Base Setup:**
- 2 Speed cards
- 2 Wit cards
- 2 Flex slots (usually Power cards with good skills)

**Target:** 50-70% Race Bonus

**Key Cards:**
- **Matikane Fukukitaru (SSR):** +30 to all stats at max level, 10% Race Bonus, Pal support stats
- **Narita Top Road (SSR):** Scales with Fan Bonus (20% Training at 200k fans), 10% Race Bonus
- **Kitasan Black (SSR):** Best Speed training despite 5% Race Bonus
- **Fine Motion (SSR):** Great Wit card with 10% Race Bonus
- **Nice Nature (SSR):** Only Wit card with 15% Race Bonus

### Wit + Guts Framework

**Base Setup:**
- 3 Guts cards
- 2 Wit cards
- 1 Flex (Speed or 4th Guts)

**Advantages:**
- Can cap Guts (1500 with base 1200 cap)
- Only need Guts Anklets from shop
- Guts raises 3 stats at once including Speed

**Disadvantages:**
- Harder to source skills
- Difficult to hit Stamina requirements for Long races

### Free-to-Play Options

**Budget Speed-Wit:** 70% Race Bonus achievable
**Budget Guts-Wit:** 60% Race Bonus achievable

## Strategy Tips

### General Race Strategy

1. **Typical Career:** 30-40 races across ~72 turns
2. **Schedule Pattern:** 2 races + 1 free turn (repeat)
3. **Race Fatigue:** Consecutive races trigger negative events

#### Consecutive Race Fatigue Table

| Consecutive Races | Mood Down (1+ Energy) | Mood Down (0 Energy) | Skin Outbreak (1+ Energy) | Skin Outbreak (0 Energy) | -10 Stats (1+ Energy) | -10 Stats (0 Energy) |
|-------------------|------------------------|----------------------|---------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| 1 Race | 0% | 15% | 0% | 4% | 0% | 0% |
| 2 Races | 0% | 33% | 0% | 8% | 0% | 0% |
| 3 Races | 60% | 100% | 15% | 25% | 0% | 0% |
| 4+ Races | 100% | 100% | 33% | 33% | 40% | 40% |

**Mitigation:** Use Cupcakes for mood, Rich Hand Cream/Miracle Cure for Skin Outbreak.

### Unique Skill Level Requirements

Trackblazer has different Unique level requirements:

| Date | Fans Required | Director Bond |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| Late Dec, Junior | 5,000+ | 19 (1 Blue) |
| Late Dec, Classic | 60,000+ | 31 (2 Blue) |
| Late Dec, Senior | 120,000+ | 51 (Green) |

### When to Train vs Race

**Prioritize Training:**
- Summer Camp periods (Level 5 facilities)
- Exceptional training turns (high friendship, rainbow training)
- When low on Coins and needing shop items

**Prioritize Racing:**
- G1 races (especially for epithets)
- When shop has high-value items
- To build Director Bond for Unique levels

### Summer Camp Strategy

**Before Summer:**
- Save Training Items (Megaphones, Ankle Weights)
- Save Energy Items (Vita, Royal Kale Juice)
- Save Reset Whistles
- Keep at least 100 Coins

**During Summer:**
- All facilities Level 5
- Use Megaphones + Ankle Weights + Whistles
- Use Good-Luck Charm for 0% failure at low energy

### Cleat Hammer Usage

- **Save 3 Master Cleat Hammers** for Twinkle Star Climax
- **Use Artisan Cleat Hammers** on G1/G2 races
- **Never use on lower tier races**

### Aptitude Requirements

**Recommended Minimums:**
- Mile: A (B acceptable with sparks)
- Medium: A (B acceptable with sparks)
- Long: C+ for Long routes
- Turf: Required for most schedules

**Spark Requirements to Raise Aptitudes:**
- B → A: 1 spark
- C → A: 4 sparks
- D → A: 7 sparks
- E → A: 10 sparks

## Advantages and Disadvantages

### Advantages

1. **More Fans:** Easy to gain fans due to frequent racing
2. **Better Parent Training:** Higher stat potential for inheritance
3. **Race Bonus Priority Reduces F2P Gap:** Easy to build strong decks with free cards
4. **No Secret Events:** Avoids difficult negative events for certain trainees
5. **Sandbox Freedom:** Complete control over race selection

### Disadvantages

1. **Races and Planning Take Time:** Each run takes 30 minutes to an hour
2. **Most Trainee Events Unavailable:** Cannot get certain skills from character events
3. **Narrow Aptitudes Disadvantaged:** Dirt-only and Long-only trainees struggle
4. **Losses Have Greater Impact:** The "MANT Domino" - losing → less coins → fewer items → weaker progression
