# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### What is Umamusume Career Planner?

Umamusume Career Planner is a comprehensive tool for planning and optimizing your Uma Musume Pretty Derby training runs. It helps you track characters, plan training strategies, manage support card decks, and make data-driven decisions with AI-powered recommendations.

### Is this an official Cygames product?

No, this is a fan-made tool created to help players optimize their gameplay. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cygames.

### Is my data safe?

Yes, your data is stored securely and is only accessible to you. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. You can export your data at any time.

### Can I use this on mobile devices?

Yes! The application is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. It's also a Progressive Web App (PWA), so you can install it on your device for offline access.

## Characters

### How many characters can I create?

There is no limit to the number of characters you can create.

### Can I edit a character's stats after creation?

Yes, you can edit character stats at any time from the character detail page.

Yes, use the "Duplicate" option from the character menu to create a copy with the same base stats.

## Training & Careers

### What's the difference between URA Finale and Unity Cup?

- **URA Finale**: Standard solo training scenario focused on individual performance
- **Unity Cup**: Team-based scenario with Spirit Burst mechanics and team synergies

### How long is a career?

A standard career is 72 turns, divided into Junior (1-24), Classic (25-48), and Senior (49-72) phases.

### Can I have multiple active careers?

Yes, but only one active career per character at a time.

### What happens if training fails?

Training failures can result in:

- No stat gains for that turn
- Potential injuries (temporary stat reduction)
- Energy loss

### How is training success calculated?

Success rate depends on:

- Current energy level
- Character mood
- Training type
- Support card effects

## Support Cards

### How do I build a deck?

1. Go to your character's deck page
2. Click on an empty slot
3. Browse or search for cards
4. Select a card to add it

### What's the best deck composition?

It depends on your goals, but generally:

- 2-3 cards matching your primary stat focus
- 1-2 cards for secondary stats
- 1 friend card for flexibility

### What are meta tiers?

Meta tiers rank cards by their overall effectiveness:

- **S+**: Best in class
- **S**: Excellent
- **A**: Very good
- **B**: Good
- **C**: Average

### How do synergies work?

Some cards have synergy bonuses when used together. The deck analyzer shows which cards synergize well.

## Skills

### How do I learn skills?

1. Obtain skill hints from support cards during training
2. Accumulate enough SP (Skill Points)
3. Go to the skill shop
4. Purchase the skill

### What's the difference between skill types?

- **Normal**: Common skills, lower cost
- **Rare**: Powerful skills, higher cost
- **Unique**: Character-specific, very powerful
- **Inherited**: From parent characters

### How do skill hints work?

Skill hints reduce the SP cost of learning a skill. Multiple hints stack for greater discounts.

### Can I remove learned skills?

No, once a skill is learned, it cannot be removed. Choose carefully!

## Races

### Which races should I enter?

Consider:

- Your character's aptitudes (distance, surface, running style)
- Current stat levels
- Race grade and competition
- Career goals

### What do race grades mean?

- **G1**: Championship races, highest prestige and rewards
- **G2**: Major races, good rewards
- **G3**: Important races, moderate rewards
- **OP**: Open races, entry-level
- **Pre-OP**: Beginner races

### How are race results determined?

Race performance depends on:

- Character stats
- Aptitude matches
- Skill activations
- Random factors

## Technical Issues

### The app is running slowly. What can I do?

Try these steps:

1. Clear your browser cache
2. Close other browser tabs
3. Disable browser extensions
4. Try a different browser

### I'm seeing an error message. What should I do?

1. Note the error message
2. Refresh the page
3. If it persists, clear cache and try again
4. Contact support with the error details

### The app doesn't work offline. Why?

Make sure you've:

1. Visited the app while online first
2. Installed it as a PWA
3. Allowed offline storage in your browser

### Which browsers are supported?

- Chrome (recommended)
- Firefox
- Safari
- Edge

Internet Explorer is not supported.
